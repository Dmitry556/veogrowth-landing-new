2026 Human-in-the-loop AI agent outbound playbook.

1 / CONTEXT

Before any outreach runs, the system spends week one learning the business it's going to represent. Founder interviews, rep calls, customer reviews, CRM patterns, competitor audit. Everything gets captured into a single operating document, typically 40 to 80 pages, that every downstream agent reads from on every run.

2 / TARGETING

The Targeting Agent sources every company in the TAM and enriches each one enough to make a real ICP decision. Every enriched company then runs against the ICP definition from the Context document, using qualitative fit logic specific to this client.

Output: 5,000 to 15,000 net-new accounts, updated weekly, owned by the client's team.

3 / RESEARCH

For each qualified account, the Research Agent runs 15 to 20 targeted searches, reads through what surfaces, and writes a brief. Typical brief runs 4,000 to 5,000 words with 20 to 25 cited sources.

Captures things like a valuation event and what it implies for timeline pressure, a rebrand that signals an operating-model change, a product launch and the specific competitor it's positioning against, an executive statement from a podcast about strategic priorities.

Output: a brief per account where every claim links back to the URL it came from.

4 / SIGNAL

Daily scan across 50+ signal types. The Context document defines which signals matter for this specific buyer. Signals that fire for a VP Sales don't fire for a Head of Research or a VP Finance.

Output: daily feed of accounts heating up, scored 0 to 100 for relevance.

5 / OUTREACH

When a signal fires on a qualified account, the Outreach Agent writes a specific email for that account, anchored in the research and the signal.

Output: one-to-one emails, sent from branded infrastructure in the client's name.

6 / MEETING

Replies get classified, timings negotiated against AEs' availability. Ambiguous ones escalate to a human. Confirmed meetings land on the client's reps' calendars. 24 hours before each meeting, a pre-call brief reaches the rep: who they're meeting, what fired, what to lead with, what to avoid.

Output: qualified meetings on calendars, with a brief attached to each one.

/ / /

Between the agents sits an operator. Agents run the automatable work. The operator handles the judgment calls: was this account actually qualified, is this reply actually a yes, does this email actually sound right for this buyer.
